Healthcare and transport assistance

Public help with healthcare costs, patient transport, disability travel concessions and local mobility schemes. Confirm the rules with the relevant public authority.

How to check and apply

  1. Choose the country and the responsible national, regional or local authority.
  2. Read the current original official notice and verify eligibility, amount and deadline.
  3. Prepare identity, residence, income, family or business evidence if the notice requires it.
  4. Submit only through the application channel named by the official authority.

Documents you may need

Identity document, proof of residence, income or family records, bank details and any additional evidence required by the programme. The exact list varies by country and scheme.

Rules differ by country

Benefit amounts, eligibility, application windows and responsible authorities vary by country, region and programme.
