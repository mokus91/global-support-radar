# Third-party materials and source obligations

Global Support Radar links to and may process information from government portals, public
datasets, APIs, RSS feeds, and other external sources. Those materials are not automatically
owned by the project.

Before redistributing or commercially reusing a source, check:
- the source's licence and terms of use;
- attribution and citation requirements;
- database rights and reuse conditions;
- API rate limits and access restrictions;
- privacy and personal-data restrictions;
- whether the source permits commercial reuse.

Software dependencies, fonts, icons, images, and other embedded third-party assets must be
listed here or in their own notices as they are audited. Do not assume that a public webpage
or government-hosted file is automatically free of all reuse conditions.

The project may claim rights only in its own original code, documentation, design, and
qualifying original selection or arrangement of information, subject to applicable law.
