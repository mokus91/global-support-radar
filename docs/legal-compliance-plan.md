# Legal and privacy readiness plan — Global Support Radar

Status: working checklist, not legal advice and not a published legal notice.
Last reviewed: 2026-11-20

## Do not publish incomplete legal notices
The operator's legal identity, contact address, contact email, legal form and actual processing practices must be confirmed before publishing final notices. Do not invent or infer these details.

## 1. Operator and service information
- [ ] Confirm whether the operator is a natural person or a registered business/organisation.
- [ ] Record the exact legal name (and representative, if applicable).
- [ ] Confirm the service address / registered office and public contact email.
- [ ] If registration or licence details apply, verify them against the relevant public register.
- [ ] State clearly that Global Support Radar is an independent information service, not a government body, grant administrator or application intermediary.

## 2. Privacy notice (GDPR)
Before drafting the final notice, inventory actual processing:
- [ ] Contact channel and any messages received (including GitHub Issues, if retained as a contact route).
- [ ] Browser storage/localStorage and the exact keys, purposes and retention.
- [ ] Hosting and server/security logs, including GitHub Pages and any provider-side logs.
- [ ] Advertising technologies and Google AdSense status/configuration.
- [ ] Analytics, embedded content, translation services or other third-party scripts, if present.
- [ ] Data recipients/processors, international transfers, retention periods and rights request channel.
- [ ] Determine lawful bases separately for each processing purpose.

## 3. Cookies and similar technologies
- [ ] Inspect actual cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage and third-party requests in a clean browser.
- [ ] Classify strictly necessary vs non-essential technologies.
- [ ] Do not activate non-essential advertising/analytics storage before valid consent where required.
- [ ] If required, implement a suitable consent management platform with accept, reject and granular settings, plus an easy way to withdraw/change consent.
- [ ] Ensure the privacy/cookie notice matches the live implementation.

## 4. Website terms and information accuracy
- [ ] Explain that programme details can change and users must verify deadlines, eligibility and application rules at the linked official source.
- [ ] Explain that the site does not submit applications, guarantee eligibility, award grants or provide legal/financial advice.
- [ ] Provide a clear correction/reporting channel.
- [ ] Explain permitted use of site content and third-party source material, respecting each source's licence and attribution requirements.

## 5. Advertising and commercial activity
- [ ] Verify AdSense site review/approval in the publisher account; code presence alone is not approval.
- [ ] Verify the required consent configuration for visitors in the EEA/UK/Switzerland, as applicable to the ad products used.
- [ ] Identify sponsored content clearly and keep editorial information separate from paid placement.
- [ ] Do not publish unverified traffic, revenue, partner or endorsement claims.
- [ ] Review hosting terms before adding paid subscriptions, paid reports or SaaS functionality.

## 6. Data-source rights register
For every source, record:
- official source URL and responsible authority;
- source type and update frequency;
- licence / terms of reuse and commercial-use status;
- attribution requirements;
- whether automated access is permitted;
- last review date and evidence.
Do not assume that public availability means unrestricted commercial reuse.

## 7. Release gate
Final legal pages may be published only after:
1. operator details are confirmed;
2. actual data flows and browser storage are inspected;
3. notices match the real implementation;
4. a qualified Hungarian/EU legal professional reviews the final documents where needed.
