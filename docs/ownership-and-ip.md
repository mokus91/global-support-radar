## Intended ownership and control

The project owner's stated intention is that one individual holds 100% of the transferable
economic rights in the original project materials, and retains sole authority to decide
whether to sell, assign, license, commercialise, or otherwise exploit the project.

No co-owner, investor, contributor, or other person is authorised by this document to sell
or license the project on the owner's behalf.

The owner's legal name and identity should be recorded in a private, signed ownership
declaration and any necessary assignment agreements. They are intentionally not published
in this repository.

## What may be protected

Subject to originality, lawful authorship, and third-party rights, project materials may
include:

- original source code and scripts;
- original website text, documentation, and graphics;
- original database selection, arrangement, structure, and metadata;
- original data-processing, validation, and automation implementations;
- original project branding and design elements.

Copyright protects original expression, not the general idea, business model, method,
algorithm, functionality, or facts as such.

## Important limits

This repository contains or may contain links to official government sources, public
records, third-party datasets, and software dependencies. The project owner does not claim
exclusive ownership of those third-party materials. Their original licences, terms of use,
database rights, attribution requirements, and reuse restrictions continue to apply.

Git commit history and repository administration are useful evidence of development and
control, but they do not by themselves prove that every right has been validly transferred
to the repository owner.

The repository's current public visibility also means GitHub's Terms of Service apply.
A public repository may be viewed and forked under GitHub's platform terms. The proprietary
notice does not remove those platform permissions.

## Rights-chain checklist

Before making an unconditional legal claim of 100% ownership or selling the project, the
owner should retain:

1. A private signed declaration identifying the natural person who owns the project.
2. Copies of relevant original source files and dated development snapshots.
3. Written assignments from any person or company that contributed protectable material
   and whose economic rights are not already held by the owner.
4. A list of third-party components, data sources, licences, and attribution obligations.
5. Evidence of control of the domain, hosting, GitHub account, and any related service
   accounts.
6. Copies of any future contractor, designer, translator, or developer agreements stating
   who owns the transferable economic rights and what rights are licensed.

## Sale and licensing authority

The project owner's intention is to retain the exclusive decision whether to:

- sell or assign all or part of the transferable rights;
- grant exclusive or non-exclusive licences;
- admit an investor or co-owner;
- transfer the domain, repository, brand, customer relationships, or operating assets.

No sale, assignment, investment, or licence is authorised merely by this document. Any
such transaction must be approved by the owner and recorded in a separate written
agreement.
