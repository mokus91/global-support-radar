# Launch validation plan

**Project:** Global Support Radar  
**Prepared:** 2026-11-20  
**Status:** Pre-launch experiment; no user demand or revenue is assumed.

## Objective

Find out whether people can use the current MVP to locate a relevant official support source, understand the limits of a result, and reach the original government notice. The first experiment should test usefulness and trust, not attempt to sell subscriptions.

## Test cohort

Recruit 10–15 people who represent different needs, for example:
- an individual or family looking for household support;
- a small business owner;
- a farmer;
- a student or researcher;
- a person looking for housing or energy support;
- a visitor outside Hungary, if feasible.

Do not collect identity documents, financial details, health information, or application credentials. Participation should be voluntary.

## Five tasks for each tester

1. Open the site on a phone and choose their country.
2. Find a relevant support category.
3. Search for one topic using a plain-language keyword.
4. Open the official source and identify where the deadline and eligibility rules are stated.
5. Explain whether the result is confirmed open, an unverified candidate, or only a source-directory link.

Do not coach the tester unless they are stuck; record where they hesitate.

## Metrics to record

| Metric | How to measure | Initial target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task completion | Tester completes tasks 1–4 without help | At least 8 of 10 |
| Time to official source | Minutes from opening site to official source | Median under 3 minutes |
| Status comprehension | Correctly distinguishes verified/open from unverified | At least 8 of 10 |
| Mobile usability | Can search and open results on their own phone | At least 8 of 10 |
| Trust | Tester can identify the source and its limitations | At least 8 of 10 |
| Return intent | Tester says they would use it again and explains why | Record, do not assume |

These are experiment thresholds, not measured results or guarantees.

## Feedback questions

Ask after the tasks:
- What were you trying to find?
- What was confusing or missing?
- Did you trust the result? Why or why not?
- Did you reach a useful official source?
- What would make you return?

Record anonymised notes only. Ask permission before quoting feedback publicly.

## Safety and quality gates before promotion

- Never describe an unverified candidate as an open grant.
- Never promise eligibility, award, or payment.
- Keep the official source link visible.
- Clearly label historic directories, archives, and research-only feeds.
- Check the current source timestamp and failure state.
- Do not collect sensitive personal data or offer to submit applications.
- If a source is stale or inaccessible, show the limitation rather than guessing.

## Decision rules

- If fewer than 8 of 10 testers can reach an official source, fix navigation and search before advertising.
- If fewer than 8 of 10 understand verification labels, improve the labels and explanations before adding more feeds.
- If users cannot find relevant current opportunities, expand and verify sources for the most requested countries/categories first.
- Do not add paid subscriptions until repeated use and a clear paid benefit are demonstrated.
- Do not estimate revenue from test interest alone; use actual retention, conversion, refund, and operating-cost data.

## What this plan does not claim

No user testing has been conducted by creating this document. No visitors, customers, subscriptions, conversion rate, or revenue are implied. Results must be collected and dated after real tests.
