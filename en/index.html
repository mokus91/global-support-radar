What the directory covers

Global Support Radar helps individuals, families, farmers, businesses, students and community organisations discover official public-support information. Explore sources for housing, family support, agriculture, business, education, energy, employment and social protection.

The directory brings together national public portals, official funding databases and selected international institutions. Coverage differs by country and topic. A listed portal is a starting point for research, not proof that a specific grant is currently open.

How to find public support

1. Choose a country

Start with the country where you live, work, study or operate a business.

2. Select a topic

Browse support areas such as housing, family benefits, farming, small business, education or energy.

3. Check the official source

Open the government or public institution website for current rules, deadlines, amounts and application steps.

Important information before applying

Programmes, eligibility rules and deadlines can change. Always confirm details on the linked official website. Global Support Radar is an independent information directory, not a government agency, grant administrator or legal adviser. It does not guarantee eligibility or funding.
