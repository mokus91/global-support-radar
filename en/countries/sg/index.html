Singapore

Official government business support and financing sources · Updated 2026-11-22

Funding is not automatic. Some schemes are loans or guarantees, not grants. Verify eligibility and current application status with the official provider.

Official programmes

EDGE Grant

Business grant launched on 30 September 2026. SMEs may receive up to 70% depending on activity, with an annual combined cap of S$100,000 per company. Verify the detailed conditions on Enterprise Singapore's website.

Enterprise Financing Scheme

Government risk-sharing scheme for bank financing covering working capital, fixed assets, trade, projects and more. It is a loan, not a non-repayable grant.

SME Cash Grant 2026

Announced for November 2026: eligible SMEs receive S$500 per local employee, capped at S$2,500 per enterprise. Verify eligibility on the official Budget page.
