Qatar

Official startup, innovation and business finance sources · QA · 2026-11-22

Important: programmes commonly require a Qatar-based or registered business, a business plan, milestones and financial assessment. Funding is not automatic and is not always a non-repayable grant.

Supports technology startups launching or expanding in Qatar. The page lists up to USD 1.1m for START and USD 5.5m for GROW, subject to milestones and eligibility.
