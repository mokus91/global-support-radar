United Arab Emirates (UAE) — Dubai support directory

Official government information on business incentives, SME support, financing and social welfare. This page links to primary sources; it is not a grant provider.

Important eligibility notice

Many UAE programmes are restricted to Emirati citizens, UAE-national-owned businesses, locally licensed companies, or selected sectors. Financing may be a loan or investment rather than a grant. A listing does not mean that a visitor is eligible or that applications are open.

Official opportunities and information

1. Dubai — SME in a Box

A government-announced platform bringing together licensing, banking, payment, logistics, telecom and other business services through partner offers. The announced potential value of up to AED 80,000 is an estimated value of savings/services depending on needs, not a cash grant.

Official announcement →

2. Dubai — funding for SMEs and startups

Overview of funding routes including Dubai Future District Fund, Dubai Future Accelerators, Mohammed bin Rashid Fund for SME and Dubai SME. Conditions vary; some programmes target Emirati founders.

Dubai Government source →

3. Dubai — 2026 business incentive packages

The announced AED 2.5 billion package includes fee deferrals and sector-specific relief. Not all measures are cash grants or universally available.

Invest in Dubai source →

4. UAE — National Programme for SMEs

Federal support for Emirati entrepreneurs and SME owners, including training, expertise, financing and procurement opportunities. Membership criteria apply.

Ministry of Economy and Tourism →

5. UAE — social welfare programmes

Official information about social welfare allowances. Eligibility depends on programme rules, citizenship and the competent local authority.

UAE Government portal →

6. UAE — National Industrial Resilience Fund

Industrial financing for selected strategic sectors, subject to project and credit requirements. This is not a general household grant.
