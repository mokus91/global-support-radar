Families and individuals

Housing and energy

Farmers and rural areas

Agricultural subsidies, rural development, equipment and climate-related support.

Businesses and work

Small-business grants, hiring incentives, training, innovation and employment support.

Education and research

Scholarships, student grants, research funding and vocational training.

Health and transport

Public healthcare assistance, mobility support and transport concessions.

How to check a programme before applying

  1. Choose the country where you meet the residence, work, study or business conditions.
  2. Open the relevant official government or public-agency source.
  3. Check eligibility, application dates, funding limits, required documents and the responsible office.
  4. Apply only through the official channel. Keep copies of submitted forms and confirmations.
Important: Global Support Radar is an independent information directory, not a government agency or grant administrator. A listed portal is not proof that a specific programme is currently open. Rules, deadlines and budgets can change. Always confirm the latest details with the responsible official institution.
